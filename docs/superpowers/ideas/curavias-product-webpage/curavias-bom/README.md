## How to use it

1. **Step 1** tells you the *page shape* to copy (Microsoft for Healthcare CD/CI).
2. **Step 2** tells you what to *build* for the app (grounded in ARCHITECTURE/PRD/SD/SECURITY/bom.yaml).
3. **Step 3** tells you what to *draw/animate* to tell the story (patient path kept).
4. **Step 4** tells you what to *write/shoot* for the website, section by section.
5. **Assets** tells you what to *download* from Brand Central.
6. `00-master-bom.csv` is the single tracker — sort by `priority`, filter by `step` or `origin`.
