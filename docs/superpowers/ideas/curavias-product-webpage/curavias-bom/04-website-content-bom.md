## 4.4 Copy production notes

- **Tone:** confident, plain, advisory. Trust refrain *"Der Mensch entscheidet."*
- **Voice rules:** "berät/Vorschlag/Vorschau" — never "entscheidet/diagnostiziert" for the AI.
- **Languages:** DE-CH primary; EN parallel copy for S1, S7, S9, S10.
- **SEO metadata:** title, meta description, OpenGraph image, structured headings (REF-DS-10).
- **Alt text:** DE + EN for every image; describe diagrams for screen readers.
- **Every factual claim** (KPIs, BVA) carries the ROM/synthetic caveat.
- **Word-count budget:** ~1,200–1,600 DE words for the single page; ~150–250 per section.
